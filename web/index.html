Key Finding: Neutral Minority Class Recovery

Model 1 suffered from severe majority-class collapse, assigning zero reviews to the neutral category (F1 = 0.0000). Setting fit_prior=False alongside reduced Laplace smoothing (alpha=0.1) restored class sensitivity, yielding a Macro F1 of 0.6762 and an operational neutral F1 of 0.3883.
